Helpful Before You Book

What Information Helps With a Chimney Enquiry?

A clear description can make the first conversation more useful. If possible, keep the following details ready when contacting Kitchen Chimney Solution.

  • The chimney brand and model number, if visible.
  • Whether the problem concerns suction, noise, lighting, controls, filters or starting.
  • How long the issue has been occurring and whether it happens every time.
  • Your locality and a nearby landmark for service-area confirmation.
  • Any previous repair details or warranty documents relevant to the appliance.

Do not open electrical components or attempt internal repairs unless qualified to do so. If you notice burning smells, sparks or unusual overheating, switch off the appliance if safe and seek appropriate assistance.

Genuine Parts and Repair Transparency

Parts availability and compatibility depend on the appliance model and supplier. Ask for the proposed part details, price and applicable warranty before approving replacement. No part is represented as manufacturer-original unless that can be substantiated.
