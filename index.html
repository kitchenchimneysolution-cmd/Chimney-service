About our service

Clear Information Before Any Work Begins

Kitchen chimneys deal with smoke, vapour and airborne grease during everyday cooking. Over time, the filter, airflow route, controls or moving components may need attention. The right next step depends on the appliance design, how it is used and the symptoms you describe.

Kitchen Chimney Solution provides a local contact point for service enquiries in Ghaziabad. Tell us whether you need cleaning, are planning a new installation, or have noticed weak suction, unusual noise, a lighting concern or another operating problem. If you know the model number, include it with your enquiry.

We discuss the reported issue and check whether a visit can be arranged. The diagnosis, estimated cost, parts availability, work scope and appointment timing should be agreed before any repair or other chargeable work starts.

Which services can I enquire about?

You can enquire about chimney cleaning, repair, installation and maintenance, including concerns such as grease build-up, weak suction, unusual noise or operating problems. Availability must be confirmed.

This website does not claim manufacturer authorisation or affiliation. If you require official manufacturer warranty service, contact the manufacturer or its listed service channel directly.

Is a visit confirmed when I submit the form?

No. The form sends a service enquiry only. A visit is confirmed after the business separately confirms the location, date, time, service scope and applicable charges.

Do you provide a 90-day warranty?

A 90-day repair service warranty is intended for eligible repair work, subject to the written warranty terms provided on the invoice or job sheet. The covered work, dates and exclusions should be confirmed before the repair is approved. It is not a manufacturer's warranty.
