Common Issues You Can Enquire About

  • Chimney suction appears weaker than usual.
  • Grease or dirt has collected on accessible filters.
  • The chimney makes unusual noise or vibrates.
  • The control panel or light is not responding.
  • The motor does not appear to run normally.
  • You need installation or routine maintenance advice.

For electrical faults, switch off the appliance and avoid opening electrical components yourself. A technician can advise on inspection.
