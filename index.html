Kitchen Chimney Cleaning, Repair & Installation

Explore common service requirements. The final work depends on the appliance model, inspection findings and the scope agreed with you.

SERVICE 01

Kitchen Chimney Deep Cleaning

Cooking fumes can leave grease and residue on filters and accessible surfaces. The cleaning approach depends on the chimney design, filter material and amount of accumulated grease. We discuss the required work before proceeding.

Share your chimney model and usage pattern when you enquire. This helps clarify the cleaning requirements and whether additional work may be needed.

Book Cleaning →
SERVICE 02

Hafele Chimney Repair

Submit a repair enquiry if your Hafele chimney is not operating normally. Common concerns include weak suction, unusual noise, controls not responding, lighting issues or an appliance that does not start.

Symptoms alone do not establish the fault. The possible repair, parts, charges and service availability should be confirmed after an appropriate assessment.

Enquire About Repair →
SERVICE 03

Filter Cleaning & Grease Removal

Filters can collect oily deposits during everyday cooking. Different models use different filter designs, so cleaning instructions and the suitable method should be checked for the particular appliance.

Avoid forcing filters out or using chemicals that are not suitable for the appliance. Tell us the issue and ask about the expected work before confirming.

Enquire About Filters →
SERVICE 04

Kitchen Chimney Installation

Installation planning may involve mounting height, wall structure, duct route, power access and the layout around the cooking area. Site conditions can affect the work and materials required.

Tell us whether you are installing a new appliance, replacing an old chimney or changing its position. Confirm installation scope and additional costs before the visit.

Enquire About Installation →
SERVICE 05

Motor, Suction & Noise Issues

Weak airflow, vibration and unexpected sounds can have different causes, including grease build-up, an obstruction, loose components or mechanical and electrical problems.

Describe when the issue occurs and whether the appliance still operates. If you notice sparks, burning smells or an electrical hazard, stop using it and seek appropriate professional assistance.

Describe Your Problem →
SERVICE 06

Preventive Maintenance

Routine attention to filters, accessible surfaces and normal appliance operation can help you identify changes that need attention. Maintenance needs depend on cooking frequency and the appliance instructions.

Share your usage pattern and any symptoms so the appropriate service scope can be discussed. Cleaning or maintenance cannot guarantee that every future fault will be prevented.
