Understand the issue

Common Chimney Problems Explained

These descriptions can help you explain what is happening when you submit your service enquiry.

↗ Weak Suction

When fumes are not being drawn away effectively, check the user-accessible filter and review the appliance instructions. Blockage, duct layout, grease build-up or a mechanical fault may be relevant. A proper assessment is needed to identify the cause.

◉ Excess Grease

Grease can accumulate on filters and accessible surfaces during everyday cooking. The required cleaning work depends on the amount of residue and the chimney's construction. Avoid harsh chemicals that are not approved for the appliance.

♫ Unusual Noise

New rattling, vibration or humming may indicate a loose part, an obstruction or a mechanical concern. Record when the sound occurs and whether the appliance still operates normally. Do not continue operation if you suspect an electrical or mechanical hazard.

⚡ Chimney Not Starting

Check only safe, user-accessible items such as the power supply and operating instructions. Do not handle exposed wiring or dismantle electrical components. Tell us the model and what happens when you try to switch the appliance on.

☼ Chimney Light Not Working

A lighting problem may relate to the lamp, control system or electrical circuit. The correct replacement and repair approach depends on the particular chimney model. Share the symptoms so the likely next steps can be discussed.

⌁ Filter or Control Concern

Filters, touch controls and buttons differ across models. Avoid forcing removable parts or spraying liquids directly onto electronic controls. Provide the model number and describe any error indication, if present.
