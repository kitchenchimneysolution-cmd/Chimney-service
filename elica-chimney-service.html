Elica Chimney Service FAQs

Helpful information before requesting a service enquiry.

Do you provide Elica chimney cleaning in Ghaziabad?

You can submit a cleaning enquiry for Ghaziabad. The exact location, appointment availability and work scope need to be confirmed before a visit.

Can I enquire about an Elica chimney with low suction?

Yes. Mention the suction problem in the form. Possible causes include dirty filters, duct restrictions or a component issue; an inspection may be needed to identify the cause.

How much does chimney repair or cleaning cost?

Charges depend on the appliance, problem, location, cleaning requirements and parts, if any. Confirm the applicable inspection or visit charge and proposed work cost before approving the service.

Do you provide genuine Elica spare parts?

Part availability and compatibility must be checked for the exact model. Confirm the part brand, price and warranty terms before authorising a replacement.

Are you an authorised Elica service centre?

No. Kitchen Chimney Solution is an independent service enquiry business and does not claim to be an authorised or official Elica service centre. For manufacturer warranty or official service, contact Elica India directly through its official website.

Will submitting this form confirm my appointment?

No. The form opens WhatsApp with your details. You must review and send the message, and the appointment is only confirmed after availability and timing are agreed.

What if my Elica chimney is still under warranty?

Check your warranty terms and contact Elica's official customer support for warranty-related service. Independent work may affect warranty coverage depending on the applicable terms.
